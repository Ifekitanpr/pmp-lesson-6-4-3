# Lesson 4.6.6 custom illustration prompts
All 14 assets use Storyset-style flat vector illustration, pure white background, no border, and brand colors only: #0B7CFF blue, #FF5A2A orange, #07152F navy, #8090A6 grey, white. No gradients, shadows, text, labels, or watermarks.

1. captain-logbook-hero — captain’s dense voyage logbook beside headquarters’ concise status view.
2. risk-communication-reveal — dense register transformed into aggregated stakeholder risk report.
3. risk-cycle-screen — Identify, Analyze, Plan Responses, Implement, Monitor feeding one report.
4. risk-cycle-report-modal — report accumulating progressively through the whole risk cycle.
5. report-communicates-screen — sponsor viewing category sources and summary metrics.
6. overall-risk-sources — technical, commercial, management, and external sources converging.
7. summary-risk-metrics — threats/opportunities, category distribution, and trend at a glance.
8. hospital-risk-trend — hospital construction regulatory exposure dropping after inspection.
9. progressive-report-screen — report repeatedly updated along the project timeline.
10. progressive-report-modal — stale frozen report contrasted with a continuously refreshed report.
11. register-report-screen — risk owner and sponsor using different documents side by side.
12. risk-register-card — risk owner managing a detailed individual risk entry.
13. risk-report-card — sponsor reviewing aggregated risk posture and trend.
14. exam-captain-report — logbook and concise status report remain active for the same voyage.
